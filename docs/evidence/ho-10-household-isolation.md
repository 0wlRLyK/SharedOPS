## Allocation and additional verification

Real invitation acceptance and create/join concurrency are owned by [HO-11](https://linear.app/home-0ps/issue/HO-11/implement-single-use-invitations-with-race-protection). Currency changes under financial-history policy are owned by [HO-50](https://linear.app/home-0ps/issue/HO-50/implement-financial-request-deduplication-and-currency-freezing). Later tasks introducing household-scoped body, relationship, read or mutation operations must enforce and test isolation for those operations. These are separately owned scope, not current HO-10 acceptance blockers.

- Production Flyway empty upgrade, populated legacy rejection with unchanged data/schema, foreign keys and active-membership uniqueness: `HouseholdMigrationIT.activeMembershipIsUniqueAndReferencesExistingRows`, `productionPortsPersistAndResolveCommittedHouseholdAndMembership` and `populatedLegacyMigrationFailsWithoutChangingRowsOrSchema` — **NOT RUN** for this candidate.

External consumers and compatibility sign-off remain **OPEN**; see the [compatibility record](ho-10-compatibility.md).

- Actor-filter and transaction-proxy bypass sensitivity require candidate-bound evidence, restoration and rerun — **NOT RUN** for this candidate.
- Local required commands `make check`, `make test`, `make verify` and changed-document formatting are **NOT RUN** for this candidate.
